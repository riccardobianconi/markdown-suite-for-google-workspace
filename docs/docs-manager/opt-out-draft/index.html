Opt Out of Draft Access

Markdown Manager for Google Docs™

This page exists for users who have been added as draft testers for Markdown Manager for Google Docs™ on the Google Workspace Marketplace and wish to remove themselves from the draft testers list.

Draft testers can install and use the unpublished (draft) version of the add-on before it is released to the public. If you no longer wish to have this access, you can request to be removed at any time.

How to opt out

To opt out of draft access, email the developer at info@riccardobianconi.it with the subject line "Opt out of draft access" from the Google account that was added as a draft tester.

You will be removed from the draft testers list within 10 business days of receiving your request. A confirmation will be sent to the same email address once the removal is complete.

After removal, your Google account will only see the publicly published version of the add-on (if any) going forward, and will no longer have access to the draft version.

Related

For general support, bug reports, or feature requests unrelated to draft access, see the Support page.

To revoke the add-on's access to your Google account entirely (regardless of draft tester status), visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Markdown Manager for Google Docs™.
